## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, deterministic) writes one plan of at most
280 characters to `/output/entry.txt`: a concrete plan an AI agent can carry
out itself to turn $100 into the most money in 30 days — selling $49
ready-to-use AI workflow packs to small online shops.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
```

## Entry text

> Sell $49 AI workflow packs (order replies, listings, ad copy) to Etsy/Gumroad shops, promoted in shop-owner Facebook groups. Day 1: publish 3 packs, post in 20 groups. $100 covers listings, domain, ads. Edge: setup video + free tweaks beat generic templates; aim 10 sales/week.

277 characters, verified identical across two runs.
